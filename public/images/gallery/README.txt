PHOTO GALLERY ASSETS
====================

To add your real photographs:
1. Copy your photos into this folder:
   - `photo-1.jpg`
   - `photo-2.jpg`
   - `photo-3.jpg`
   etc.

2. In `src/config/loveConfig.ts`, you can update `defaultPhotoMemories` with your photo paths (`/images/gallery/photo-1.jpg`) and custom captions!

3. You can also directly upload photos from your device using the "নতুন স্মৃতি যোগ করুন" ("Add New Memory") button in the live website gallery!
