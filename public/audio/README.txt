ROMANTIC AUDIO ASSETS
======================

To add your own background music or personal voice recording:

1. Background Music:
   Place an MP3 file named `romantic-music.mp3` in this folder (`/public/audio/romantic-music.mp3`).
   Note: If no audio file is provided, the website automatically plays an ethereal, relaxing Web Audio synthesizer chime so there are zero missing-audio errors!

2. Personal Voice Message:
   Record a short sweet voice note (e.g. from your phone) and place it here as `voice-message.mp3` (`/public/audio/voice-message.mp3`).
   Your beloved will be able to click "আমার কথা শুনবে? ❤️" to hear your real voice!
